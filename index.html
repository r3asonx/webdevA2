Type of automobiles

There are many type of performance category, but I'll be focusing on those that are commonly found in motorsports. Click on the images to hear what they sound like!

Sports

A sports car is designed to be enjoyable to drive. Highly responsive handling, quick acceleration, and a lower center of gravity. It does not have to be extremely expensive or powerful

Typical Horsepower: 180-450 HP

TLDR: Fun handling and driving.

Supers

An exotic, high-performance car designed for exception speed, handling and striking design. Often features advanced engineering and premium materials.

Typical Horsepower: 600-900 HP

TLDR:Extreme performance and striking looks.

Hypers

The pinnacle of automotive performance, showcasing the latest technology, extreme sped and limited production. Represents the highest level of engineering and exclusivity.

Typical Horsepower: 1,000-2,000+HP

TLDR: The highest level of automotive performance, cutting-edge technology and very limited productions.

Type of engines

Honda S2000

The Honda S2000 or S2K for short, is powered by the legendary F20C engine. It is a naturally aspirated 2.0-liter, inline-4 engine. Producing 237 to 247 Horsepower. It also boast a staggering 9,000 RPM redline.

The F20C, produces 247 hp for the Japanese Domestic Market, while the US/EU Market get's a 237 Hp version.

Features Honda's VTEC system, which engages aggressively around 6,000 RPM. The engine is known for it's "split personality". While on low RPMs, the engine provides low torque, but once it reaches a high enough RPM, a surge of power kicks in.

Lamborghini Aventador LP 700-4

The Lamborghini Aventador LP700-4, powered by the L539, 6.5-liter, naturally aspirated V12 engine, produces 700 Horsepower.

Paired with a 7-speed ISR single-clutch transmission.

McLaren P1

The McLaren P1, considered to be the Holy Trinity of hypercars. Along side the Porsche 918 Spyder and the Ferrari LaFerrari.

Powered by a 3.8L twin-turbocharged V8 with a single electric motor, produces 903 Horsepower.

The M838T Engine redlines at 8500 RPM, but 80% of the engine's torque is available as low as 2000RPM.
